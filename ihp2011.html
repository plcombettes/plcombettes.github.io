OPTIMIZATION, GAMES, AND DYNAMICS

November 28-29, 2011
Institut Henri Poincaré
Amphithéâtre Hermite
11, rue Pierre et Marie Curie
Paris 5ème



  • Objective: The aim of the conference is to bring together researchers and scientists to discuss advances in optimization theory, game theory, and dynamical systems, the interconnections between these disciplines, and their applications in areas such as control theory, biology, the calculus of variations, learning theory, transportation theory, statistics, economics, and inverse problems. The conference is organized around 12 plenary talks and its language is English.
